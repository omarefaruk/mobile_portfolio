Privacy Policy – The Noble Quran

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Android app The Noble Quran (package name com.lifestudio.thenoblequran), published by Life Studio ("we", "us"), handles information. We have designed the app to respect your privacy.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The app does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address or location, and does not contain advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following on your device only, so it can work offline and remember your settings:

  • Chapters (surahs) you choose to download, so you can read them without an internet connection.
  • Your preferred text size.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Internet access and third-party services

The app connects to the internet to download the Quran text and translation from a third-party service, the Quran API (quranapi.pages.dev). As with any website or online service, that provider may receive standard technical information such as your device's IP address when the app makes a request. We do not send any personal information to this service, and we do not receive or store any information from these requests. The provider's own policies apply to their service.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
